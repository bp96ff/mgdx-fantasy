Privacy Policy

mgdxfantasy.com is a fan site for the Microgen Fantasy Football leagues (MGDX), run by the league’s managers. Anyone can read it without an account. Accounts are only for current managers, and this page explains what an account stores and why.

What we collect

Only when a manager signs in:

  • Sign-in details. Your email address. If you sign in with Google, also the name and profile picture on your Google account. If you use a password, it’s stored scrambled (hashed), and no one can read it.
  • Your manager link. Which league manager your account belongs to, and when you entered your claim code. Attempts to enter a code are logged with a time, to stop guessing.
  • Sign-in records. The sign-in service keeps basic security logs, such as when you signed in and from what IP address.
  • What you post. Your posts, reactions, team logo and any pictures you upload. These are public on the site. Deleting a post deletes its picture too.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on. For each phone or browser you turn them on in, the address its push service gives it, and which kinds of notifications you want. Turning them off on a device, or signing out there, deletes that device’s address.

The site has no ads, analytics or tracking. Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you stay signed in; signing out removes it. It also keeps a copy of the pages you’ve opened, so the app still opens without a signal.

How it’s used

Only to sign you in, show which team is yours, and let members and moderators use the site’s members-only features. We don’t sell or share your information, and we don’t send email except for account messages you ask for, like confirming your address or resetting your password.

Who stores it

  • Supabase runs the sign-in service and the database that holds accounts, posts and uploaded pictures.
  • GIPHY runs the Main Blog’s GIF search. What you type in that search box goes to GIPHY, and GIFs picked from it load from GIPHY.
  • Google handles “Continue with Google” if you choose it, and serves the site’s fonts.
  • Resend delivers account emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla (whichever made your phone or browser) deliver notifications, if you turn them on. What a notification says passes through their push service, encrypted.

League scores, rosters and team names come from ESPN Fantasy and are shown to everyone, with or without an account.

What other managers see

Members can see which managers have linked accounts. Your email address isn’t shown on the site.

Deleting your account

Ask a league moderator (Bryce, Kat or Laith) to delete your account. We’ll remove it and its manager link. Accounts of managers who leave the league can be deleted the same way.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
